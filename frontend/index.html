The whole sky, twice a year

SPHEREx circles Earth from pole to pole, scanning one thin strip of sky on every orbit. In about six months the strips close into a complete map, and the survey starts over.

0% of the sky scanned

02 / 03

Infrared light, split 102 ways

Six detectors cover 0.75 to 5.0 µm. A filter that changes colour across each one sorts the light into 102 narrow channels, far past what eyes can see.

0.75 µm channel 1 of 102

03 / 03

Same patch, different night

Line up every visit to one patch and play them back. Stars hold still. Whatever moves or changes brightness gives itself away.
